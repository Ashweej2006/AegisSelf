## Brand & Style
The design system establishes an environment of uncompromised cryptographic assurance combined with restorative clinical vitality. Crafted for privacy-conscious individuals and proactive health optimizers, the interface channels institutional-grade zero-trust data protection into a calm, personal telemetry sanctum. 

The aesthetic is **Clinical Modern with Cryptographic Precision**—grounded in structural discipline, ultra-refined borders, luminous high-clarity surfaces, and delicate frosted overlays. Visual friction is eliminated to inspire confidence; telemetry reads like an advanced aerospace dashboard re-engineered for biological wellness. Interfaces balance deep architectural stability with responsive emerald vital signs and crystalline cyan data streams, reinforcing agency, privacy, and continuous baseline equilibrium.

## Colors
The palette leverages a high-contrast, hyper-legible foundation balanced with biological status accents:

- **Primary (`#0F172A` - Deep Vault Slate):** The cornerstone of zero-trust authority. Used for structural navigation, foundational text, and high-security state affordances.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` - Emerald Vitality):** Signifies biological health, verified cryptographic states, affirmative telemetry, and baseline recovery.
- **Tertiary (`#06B6D4` - Telemetry Cyan):** Designates active biometric pipelines, sensor data streams, cryptographic handshakes, and interactive metrics.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Core):** Manages secondary labels, axis lines, inactive states, and structural hierarchy.
- **Canvas & Card Architecture:** The canvas sits at `#F8FAFC`, paired with pure `#FFFFFF` elevated cards and `#E2E8F0` micro-borders to maintain clinical crispness without visual fatigue.

## Typography
The typographical hierarchy enforces mathematical precision alongside human approachability:

- **Display & Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers clean geometric curvature with modern warmth. Headlines command authority while avoiding excessive coldness.
- **Narrative & System Copy (Inter):** High x-height and neutral grotesque terminals guarantee effortless scan-reading of clinical recommendations and privacy protocols.
- **Telemetry & Cryptographic Labels (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced with tabular figures to render continuous biometric feeds, hash signatures, timestamps, and metric readouts with zero layout jitter.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a 12-column dynamic fluid grid on desktop (`1280px+`), an 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1279px`), and a 4-column compact grid on mobile (`< 768px`).

Grid alignment uses explicit modular rhythm:
- Outer margins compress gracefully on smaller screens (`margin-mobile: 1.25rem`) to maximize telemetry visibility while avoiding border collision.
- Component-internal layouts follow a strict 4px/8px micro-system using `space-*` tokens. Telemetry clusters and card interiors prioritize spatial breathing room to prevent cognitive fatigue during dense data inspection.
- Content reflows seamlessly: desktop side-by-side biometric visualizers collapse into vertically stacked progressive disclosures on mobile.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed through optical clarity, surface translucency, and low-contrast perimeter definitions rather than heavy structural drop shadows:

- **Base Level (Canvas):** Flat `#F8FAFC` foundation.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a hairline `#E2E8F0` border, paired with an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive & Floating Panels):** Semi-translucent panels (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`) with a `12px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) and a subtle inner keyline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` inset). Supported by elevated ambient diffusion: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Cryptographic Enclaves):** Opaque surfaces bordered with `#CBD5E1` and an anchor shadow tinted with Slate Navy: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes
A roundedness factor of `2` (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline) is deployed across all core containers. This configuration strikes the balance between clinical geometry and ergonomic touch targets:

- Standard components (buttons, input fields, badges) adopt `rounded` (`0.5rem`).
- Content modules and elevated telemetry cards scale to `rounded-lg` (`1rem`).
- Enclave containers, modals, and prominent sensor dashboards scale to `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`).
- Status badges and biometric tracking nodes leverage full pill rounding (`rounded-full`) to communicate kinetic liveliness and system responsiveness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Zero-Trust Action):** Solid `#0F172A` background, white label, `rounded` (8px), with crisp hover transitioning to `#1E293B`. Interactive micro-feedback includes a subtle scale transform (`0.99`).
- **Vitality / Verification Action:** Solid `#10B981` background, `#FFFFFF` text, paired with an ambient emerald glow on focus.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, `1px` solid `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` text; shifts to `#F1F5F9` on hover.

### Telemetry Chips & Verification Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), featuring monospace metadata (`JetBrains Mono`). 
- Health status chip: Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`, text `#047857`, framed with a continuous `1px` border of `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`. Features a pulsing 6px indicator beacon.
- Telemetry stream chip: Background `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1)`, text `#0E7490`, with `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)` outline.

### Input Fields & Secret Keys
- Input frames utilize `#FFFFFF` surfaces with an `#E2E8F0` hairline border and `rounded` geometry.
- Focused state applies an immediate border transition to `#06B6D4` paired with an optical outer ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)`.
- Key entry inputs use `JetBrains Mono` with wider kerning for error-free cryptographic entry.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Radios
- Square-curved checkboxes (`rounded`, 4px) and circular radios with `#E2E8F0` resting frames.
- Selected states fill with `#0F172A` or `#10B981` using sharp SVG confirmation vectors.

### Cards & Biometric Vault Enclaves
- `#FFFFFF` background with 16px (`rounded-lg`) corner radii and a `1px` solid `#E2E8F0` border.
- Vault cards incorporate a two-tier structural header: a human-readable title in `Plus Jakarta Sans` juxtaposed with a monospaced hash or sync status in `JetBrains Mono`.

### Specialized Enclave Components
- **Telemetry Sparkline Rails:** Compact micro-charts integrated directly into cards, rendered in `#06B6D4` with vertical scanning scrubbers.
- **Zero-Trust Seal Banner:** Frosted indicator strip with a lock icon, dynamic latency metric, and end-to-end encryption confirmation.
